Tumhari appsettings.json mein "Email" block ye hona chahiye (Port number hai, quotes nahi):

  "Email": {
    "Host": "smtp.gmail.com",
    "Port": 587,
    "User": "tumhara@gmail.com",
    "AppPassword": "16 letter app password",
    "FromEmail": "tumhara@gmail.com",
    "FromName": "Aurelle Perfume",
    "ReplyTo": "",
    "CcEmail": "tumhara@gmail.com",
    "BccEmail": "",
    "TestToEmail": ""
  },

- CcEmail: sirf ORDER email ki copy. OTP wali email mein CC kabhi nahi lagta.
- TestToEmail: khali rakho. Bharoge toh saari emails (OTP bhi) sirf us ek inbox mein jayengi.
- Gmail SMTP "From" ko login wale Gmail se badalne nahi deta.
- AppPassword kisi ko mat bhejna aur GitHub pe mat daalna.
